# Research and Outreach Rules

## Source order

1. Company website, product/service documentation, official careers pages.
2. Company reports, filings, official news, and executive statements.
3. Government, regulatory, standards, and industry-primary sources.
4. Reputable secondary sources only for context or corroboration.

Use current sources when facts may change. Cite close to the claim. Do not collect personal contact details during company research; contact discovery is a separate authorized workflow.

## Target screen

Score each 0–2: recent public trigger, visible workflow, measurable effect, three quality sources, realistic AI/human mechanism, public-data artifact feasibility, candidate fit, relevant owner role, manageable privacy/brand risk, and company-specificity.

- `16–20`: full microsite.
- `12–15`: short email plus one-page brief.
- `<12`: research or STOP.

When Agent Reach is available, its doctor still requires explicit authorization because it may inspect local config, probe authenticated tools or external services, and register a skill. After authorization, run `aomk reach doctor --authorize-upstream --json` and treat the reported route as guidance, not proof of live retrieval. Record original source URLs and dates; authenticated research channels require their own authority.

The replacement test is mandatory: if changing only the company name leaves the proposal credible, it is not personalized.

## Outreach contract

Draft 70–110 words:

1. one specific company signal;
2. one explicitly tentative workflow hypothesis;
3. the microsite as a sourced work sample;
4. the candidate's desired role;
5. one 15-minute reality-check CTA;
6. a simple opt-out.

Do not use fake `Re:` subjects, false urgency, tracking pixels, attachments on first contact, guessed addresses, automated sequences, or hidden commercial intent. Sending remains a separate human decision after checking the recipient's jurisdiction, provider rules, and suppression state.
